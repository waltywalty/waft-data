Asia-open gold · research round 13 · August 2026

Eleven more correlation partners, one conclusion

The deployed rule stands aside when the 20-day gold/AUDUSD correlation is above 0.5 — and it has been standing aside for weeks. The commission: gate the same breakout on other macro partners (silver, the dollar, EUR, JPY, GBP, CHF, CAD, CNY, oil, the S&P 500, 10-year yields) and see whether any of them adds edge or, better, rescues trades the AUD gate skips. Three questions were pre-registered before any gating: Q1 does any partner beat AUD on the full set; Q2 does any partner find a tradeable subset among AUD-skipped days; Q3 does a second gate stacked on AUD improve per-trade quality. Every cell counted: 416 cells in the ledger.

Verdict: negative on all three questions. The best cell in the whole grid (t +2.82) is matched by a random circular-shift search half the time (permutation median +2.55, p = 0.21). Every rescue cell flips sign between halves. The stacked gates that look good both halves are matched by random series ANDed onto AUD (p = 0.30). No change to the deployed rule, and no frequency gain exists in this direction.

01Data and provenance

Daily FX from the FRED mirror on GitHub (through Dec 2025), silver / WTI / 10-year yields from Alpha Vantage dailies, S&P 500 from our own verified intraday file, gold from the same broker-EET daily closes the deployed filter uses. The synthetic dollar index is the DXY basket re-weighted without SEK. Sanity signs all landed where macro says they should: silver most gold-correlated (+0.45 mean), the dollar index negative (−0.42), yields negative (−0.34), oil and equities weak (+0.14). The AUD control reproduced the deployed set exactly: 652 trades, PF 1.320, t +2.54. Base (no gate): 1062 trades, PF 1.201, t +1.83.

03Q2 — the skipped days stay dead

The 410 trades the AUD gate skips are collectively edge-free (PF 1.020, t -0.33). For each partner, the best cell on this subset only — and every single one is negative in 2020–23 and positive in 2024–25. That is not a filter finding tradeable days; that is 2024–25 being a gold bull. A gate that only works in the half where everything worked is no gate.

04Q3 — stacked gates are selection, not signal

Second gates ANDed onto the deployed set (base: 652 trades, PF 1.320, t +2.54). Two cells — silver and CNY — improve both halves, which is exactly why the permutation test matters: ANDing a random circularly-shifted regime series onto the real AUD gate and keeping the best cell reaches t +2.78 at the median and beats the observed +2.94 30% of the time. Dropping a third of the trades for a quality bump that selection alone explains — and that runs against the round's goal of more frequency — is a trade we decline.

05What this settles

The round strengthens the round-12 meta-law rather than the strategy: the edge is gated by one macro state — loosely, “the dollar factor is not driving gold today” — and AUDUSD is already an adequate window onto it. Ten other windows onto the same state neither sharpen it (Q1), extend it (Q2), nor refine it beyond chance (Q3). The frequency we have is the frequency the edge has. Droughts, including the current one, are the filter doing its job — the playbook's drought table stands unchanged.

Method: 20-day rolling correlation of daily log returns (yield uses simple differences), calendar-reindexed, lagged one day; trades = 60-minute Asia-open breakout, 2× range stop, 16:00-NY exit, $0.30 round-trip cost plus $0.30 stop slippage; halves split at 2024-01-01. Multiplicity: 416 cells, max-statistic circular-shift permutation (300 + 500 draws). Data files are not committed; sources are scripted in fetch_data.sh and run_corrpartners.py.
