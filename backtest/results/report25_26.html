The auction is part of why our rule works, not a second trade.

03Round 26b — “surely we can build something on it”: no, and here is exactly why

The fair challenge, tested with the two honest levers left. First the framing stat the t-scores hide: the candle's directional hit rate is 50.8% (49.8% in 2020-23). The statistics were confident because n = 1,267 days, not because any single day is predictable — the same power-analysis lesson the Medallion research taught in round 24: a 50.75%-grade edge is monetizable only at thousands of trades per year and near-zero cost.

  • Magnitude does not help: the quintile gradient is a hump (−0.69 / −0.47 / +0.98 / +0.94 / −0.06), not a slope — the biggest candles carry less forward information, and the top quintile is negative in 2020-23. “Trade only the strong signals” selects exhaustion.
  • Even free execution fails: at zero cost the one-trade-per-day version earns t +1.22 over five years — statistically indistinguishable from nothing. The information is real in aggregate and unreachable one position at a time.
  • The construction that DOES monetize this session already exists: the deployed rule. It waits for the range (which contains the auction) to actually break, on days the regime gate says gold is trading on its own flows — and that concentration is what turns ~5bps of diffuse daily bias into +$1.70/oz of tradable expectancy. The auction candle is a description of the engine, not a second engine.
